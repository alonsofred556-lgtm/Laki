Local keys. Simple control.

Wagon-style fast-wallet mode creates the key in this browser, encrypts it with your password, and signs only from this page. Reloading or locking clears the decrypted key from memory. Automated bot custody remains a separate audited-signer path.

! The secret is validated and encrypted in this browser only. It is never sent to Coni and cannot activate automated trading by itself.
